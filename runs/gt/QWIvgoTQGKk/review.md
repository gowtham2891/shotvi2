## Episode in brief

Day one of the marriage. He expects chai, a lunch carriage and dinner; Amrutha can't cook. His father reveals that one cooking lie cost him 4 years of silence from his son. Her mother's 'Idea' is to secretly cook everything (dinner, pre-packed dosas) while Amrutha keeps nearly giving herself away. The romance runs alongside: toenail painting, the 'send-off hug' deal, 'This is why I love short girls'. Cliffhanger: he insists on watching her fry mirchi bajji after bragging to everyone.

## How I cut (founder option 2)

Each clip is 1-2 continuous stretches (`time_segments`, the real cut, including silent beats and the reaction held after the last line). Cuts drop whole chunks, between lines and on shot changes where possible; no words are trimmed inside a stretch. `pieces` = the words inside each stretch. Speaker ids were ignored; who speaks comes from frames and content.
