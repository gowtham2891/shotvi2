## Plot beats
- **Setup:** Amrutha, short and rejected at four bride-viewings, cannot cook. The new groom wants only one thing: a wife who cooks.
- **Conflict:** her mother lies ("వచ్చు బాబు"), the Pulla Reddy sweets cover-up; Amrutha tries twice to confess and is cut off/charmed.
- **Turn:** he wins her with the stool gesture and his "food = marriage" talk; she says yes without confessing; they marry.
- **Cliffhanger:** in their new home, with the mother promised "eat your daughter's cooking any time", he declares he cannot tolerate lies or cheating. The lie is now a time bomb.
