# j14Om1SaFYE: Couple Goals Ep 1, gold review

Full map: `runs/gt/j14Om1SaFYE/map.md`. Frames: `runs/gt/j14Om1SaFYE/frames/` (5s contact sheets for the whole episode, 2s sheets at 4:26-5:20, 8:02-8:46, 17:40-18:20).

## Episode in brief
- **Amrutha** (Sushma Gopal): short, rejected at four bride-viewings, cannot cook. **Her mother** covers for her with lies.
- **The groom** (Mohit Pedada): motherless, grew up on hostel food, wants only a wife who cooks. With **his father** at the viewing, and **a friend** at the office.
- Beats: the viewing ('మీకు వంట వచ్చా?' / 'వచ్చు బాబు', the Pulla Reddy sweets lie). Kitchen-disaster flashback. Private talk: the height issue and the **stool gesture** (8:02-8:46, no dialogue); she drops her confession. He tells his friend he wants 'food made with love'. She tries to confess again; he says 'wife వంటగదికి పరిమితం కాదు' and gives the 'marriage = full meals' speech. Café: gongura/henna mix-up, 'పిండి family'. She says yes and they marry.
- **Cliffhanger:** after the wedding he promises mom she can eat her daughter's cooking any time, then rages on a call that he cannot tolerate lies or cheating. Bride and mother exchange panicked looks (18:04-18:18).
