Find every shot change

ffmpeg scans the video and marks each cut. Later, every clip starts and ends on one of these, so a clip never shows a split-second flash of the next shot.

ffmpegfree
4

Number every line

The transcript becomes numbered lines, with sound tags as lines of their own. Gemini only ever answers with these numbers, never with timestamps; code turns numbers into exact times.

codefree

5

Fix misheard words

Gemini flags words Scribe was unsure of, reads the episode for words that don't fit, then re-listens to just those audio snippets. Fixes are stored as a layer on top; Scribe's transcript is never overwritten.

6

Find moments, payoff first

Gemini reads the whole episode and looks for beats that land: a punchline, a reveal, a quotable line, an emotional peak. For each one it names the payoff line, then works back to the setup the payoff needs. Tap a block to see its payoff.

7

Cut each clip

Code turns each moment into exact cuts with your rules: start on the first spoken word (at most 0.3 s before it), end on the payoff, then hold on the reaction until the next shot change (2–8 s). Here is the saree-gift clip, zoomed in.

8

Review each clip

Gemini scores every clip as cut: hook, standalone, payoff, overall. This is the weak step. Next to its scores are yours: it rated the two clips you would reject as highly as the ones you would post.
