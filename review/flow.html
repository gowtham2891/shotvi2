no AI here

The episode video and an ElevenLabs Scribe (scribe_v2) transcript made earlier with speaker labels.

Goes in

  • YouTube episode → scribe_dataset/<id>/<id>.mp4 (+ _audio.wav)

Comes out

1 · Word fix (ASR correction layer)

AI≈ ₹2–4

Finds words Scribe misheard and proposes corrections, without touching the original transcript (a separate layer).

Goes in

  • The Scribe words; low-confidence words flagged by code
  • Call A (text): all lines, flagged words marked word(?)
  • Call B (audio): only the suspect lines' audio + 3 lines of text around them

Comes out

  • runs/asr_fix/<id>/corrections.json: word index → corrected word, accepted only when the model hears it in its own re-listen
Code
shotvi/segment/asr_fix.py
Known gap
Humming can be transcribed as words (HM L0113), parked.
↓

2 · Speaker fix

AI≈ ₹2

Scribe's speaker labels are often wrong (two people merged, one person split). This reads the transcript in three overlapping parts and gives each line a speaker key, a name when the dialogue reveals it, and a confidence. Required before Stage 1A.

Goes in

  • Transcript lines (with word fix applied) in 3 parts with overlap

Comes out

  • runs/speaker_fix/<id>.json: per line key / name / confidence, cast list
Model
Gemini 3.8 Flash, thinking low

3 · Stage 1A: episode facts (measures only)

code only₹0

Builds the numbered line table every later step uses (L0001, L0002 …): speech lines and sound lines in time order. It measures and decides nothing (your rule, 3 Nov: code only measures; the model decides scenes).

Goes in

  • Scribe sentences + sound events, word fix, speaker fix

Comes out

  • Lines: id, start, end, text, speaker key/name, sound tag class (laugh, music, silence…), zero-length fillers marked
  • Gap table: every pause between lines, what filled it
  • CG: 404 lines (348 spoken) · HM: 464 lines (437 spoken)

AI1 text call₹2.6

Reads the whole transcript (no video) and works out the people: who each speaker key is, merges (two keys = one person), roles, and stretches that are not story (ads, promos). It also proposes scenes, but its scenes are no longer used: the video step copied them when we sent them (3 Nov), so the video step now finds scenes itself.

Comes out

5 · Video step: who talks, who is on screen, and the scenes

AI1 call, whole video₹13–17

Gemini watches the whole episode (low quality, 1 frame/s, with sound) with the transcript and 1B's people marked as a guess to check. For every spoken line it says who talks and who is on screen, describes each person's look, and splits the episode into scenes. Its answer is final (no separate naming check). Names held at 94–99% on our checks; video-found scenes were the most accurate in your scene review.

Goes in

  • Whole episode video (480p, low resolution ≈ 100 tokens/s)
  • PART 1 people from 1B (as a guess)
  • PART 2 the transcript in time order (no scenes sent)
  • Clip-use context ("these scenes are used to cut clips")

Comes out

  • Per spoken line: who talks, who is on screen
  • People: name, also-known-as, role, gender, look
  • Scenes: first/last line, where, who, why it is a scene, why it ends, what comes after
  • CG 17 scenes · HM 10 scenes
Example scene (HM)

 "where": "Hansini's House", "who": ["E", "G"],
 "why": "Hansini and her mother Paddu talking in the living room and sharing wine",
 "end_why": "Night conversation ends as mother encourages her to give Aravind a chance"}

6 · Stage 2: scene notes (facts, no judgement)

AI1 call, whole video₹12–14

One call over the whole episode describes every scene as facts. Your decision (3 Nov): no payoffs, hooks or ratings here, because any judgement would steer Stage 3. One call for all scenes (not one per scene), because clips can stretch across scenes.

Goes in

  • Whole episode video
  • People with their looks (from the video step)
  • Transcript split by the video step's scenes, with who is on screen per line

Comes out

  • The episode's story in a paragraph
  • Per scene: story, situations (line ranges), mood, reactions (after which line), visual moments and silences (with times, music), what a new viewer needs to know, links to other scenes, relationships

7 · Stage 3 find: the clips, scene by scene

AI1 cache + 1 call per scene₹32–39

Works like a Telugu regional editor, not from rules: for each scene, it gives the clips whose payoff lands in that scene (a punchline, reveal, conclusion, emotional peak or cliffhanger). A clip may start in an earlier scene. Earlier this was two steps (find moments, then shape them); the second step copied the first one's lines, so on 4 Nov you merged them into this one step.

How the episode is sent once: the whole video + people + story + all scene notes + the transcript are stored at Google once (an explicit cache, ≈ 145k tokens for CG, ≈ 177k for HM, kept at most 40 min, deleted at the end). Each scene call reads it at a tenth of the normal price and sends only the instructions and "the scene to search: Scene N".

Goes in (cached once)

  • Whole episode video
  • PART 1 people (role, look)
  • PART 2 the episode's story + every scene's notes
  • PART 3 transcript by scene: id [start-end] who (on screen: …) | words

Goes in (each call)

  • The editor instructions (prompts/stage3_clips.md)
  • "The scene to search: Scene N (times, lines)"

Comes out (per clip)

  • payoff line + its words + type
  • start line + first word + why it starts here
  • end line + last word + hold_until (m:ss) + what it ends on
  • cuts (≤ 3 whole-line chunks), cold open (or none)
  • hook, title, why it works, emotion, standalone/promo, spoiler

Code then

  • Finds the quoted words' times, builds the played pieces (cold open first, cuts left out)
  • Checks: the payoff plays in the clip, hold not into the next speech, ≤ 180 s. A failed check re-asks once with the errors.
Example found clip (HM c07, 79 s) 
payoff L0406 "దండనండి." (punchline, standalone)
start  L0372 "అప్పుడప్పుడు"  why: Paddu advises Aravind about Hansini's soft nature and encourages him to keep trying.
end    L0406 "దండనండి." hold_until 21:54.5
       why: Aravind folds his hands in panic and flees after being freaked out by the mother's sudden full support.
cuts: none · cold open: none
title: వాళ్ళ అమ్మ Support నాకేనా?!
emotion: hilarious confusion and awkwardness
What your ratings showed: CG clips match your approved ones well (8/8 covered in two full runs; 10 Yes / 3 Maybe / 1 No on the first). But the step asks for a lot at once while watching 20+ minutes at 1 frame/s: it almost never cuts (3 of 19 clips), its end holds are seconds too short, and in HM's long scenes it joins two moments into one long clip (c04 140 s = your wine clip + "the thief is mom" clip). That is why step 8 exists.

8 · Stage 3 edit: the editor tightens each clip

new, testing on HMAI1 call per clip≈ ₹3.6 / clip

One call per found clip. Gemini watches only that clip's stretch (8 s before to 15 s after) at 2 frames/s, so looks, nods and eye-rolls are visible. Building on the found clip is this step's job (it edits it), so it gets the found start, end and payoff.

Goes in

  • Video of the stretch only, its clock starting at 0:00
  • PART 1 people · PART 2 the scene's story + what a new viewer needs
  • PART 3 the found moment: where it lands, rough start and end, why it works
  • PART 4 transcript of the stretch on the video's own clock (so the times it writes match what it sees)

Comes out

  • Tighten: up to 3 cuts of whole lines (side talk, repeats, small talk, dead air, song bridge), each with a reason
  • Split into two clips when the stretch holds two moments
  • start / end (may move), how long to hold on the reaction, cold open
  • hook, title, why, emotion, standalone/promo, spoiler
Example: HM c07 before and after (79 s → 41 s) 
found:  starts L0372 · no cuts · 79 s
edited: starts L0383 "మా"  why: Starts right on Paddu offering maternal insight on Hansini's nature.
        cut L0387..L0393  why: Cuts awkward goodbye pleasantries to tighten the pace straight into
                               Aravind's internal monologue.
        ends L0406 "దండనండి."  why: Ends on Aravind's hilarious panic-bow as he quickly beats a retreat
                                    after getting her unexpected blessing.
        title: అత్తగారి Support దొరికితే చాలు!   · 41 s
Result on HM (₹46 + ₹11.7 redo): first run, 4 of 8 clips came back; in the other 4 Gemini named lines by their words instead of their ids (L0123). After the fix, all 4 came back. Not tightened: c04 stays 138 s with no cut and no split (the clip you said needs internal cuts), c05 156 s, c02 102 s, c03 56 s. Ends now hold longer where it saw a reaction (c02, c03 hold 10.6 s).

9 · Cut the clips and your review

code only₹0

Every clip is cut into its own video file exactly as it would be posted (cold open first, cuts left out), then shown on a rating page. Your ratings are the measure; approvals and hand-made inputs never feed the pipeline.

Goes in

  • The clips' played pieces (times)
  • The source mp4

Comes out

  • review/clips_vN/<id>/cNN.mp4 (ffmpeg join, 720p, 0.1 s before the first word and 0.3 s after the last so no word is clipped)
  • review/stage3_clips_vN.html: Yes / Maybe / No + note per clip → you download → data/human/ratings_stage3_vN_*.csv
  • Check against your approved clips (data/gold/<id>.json): score_stage3.py

Rules this flow follows (your decisions)

  • No hard editing rules in code. The AI decides clip edges, cuts, holds and cold opens; code only measures, looks up times, checks the format, cuts video and enforces the 3-minute maximum.
  • Never hand the AI an answer it can copy. Seen three times: scenes sent to the video step were copied; 3a's lines were copied by 3b; scene-by-scene asking pulls clips toward whole scenes. Each step gets facts, not decisions (the edit step is the exception on purpose: editing the found clip is its job).
  • Think like a Telugu regional editor. The hook can be anywhere; a cliffhanger is one way to pull viewers to the episode, not the only one; clips under about 20 s are not posted for these series.
  • Web series: mostly continuous, at most ~3 cuts, each a whole chunk between lines, never words inside an actor's line.
  • Your approved clips and ratings are only for measuring; they never feed the pipeline. Paid runs need your ₹ approval first.

Open problems and parked ideas

  • End holds from the find step are seconds too short (Gemini names the right beat but times it badly at 1 frame/s). The edit step watches closely; a separate "ending check" is the fallback idea. The "pick a sound line" version is parked (it misses silent nods and looks).
  • Runs vary: the hostel-food clip (approved Yes) was found in two full runs and missed in the v7/v8 answer for scene 8.
  • Long scenes: the find step joins neighbouring moments (HM c04, c05); the edit step is asked to split them.
  • Parked: HM ad lines named Hansini · humming transcribed as words (L0113) · a person seen from behind counts as on screen · Surya merges.
