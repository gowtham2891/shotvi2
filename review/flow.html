New since the last version of this page (built, not run yet):
  • Every spoken word now has a number. Gemini answers with numbers ("starts at word 741, ends at word 759") instead of copying words. Copying caused failed answers and re-asks.

0 · The episode and its transcript

no AI₹0 here
What it does
We download the episode. ElevenLabs Scribe writes down every word with its time and guesses who speaks (speaker_0, speaker_1 …). It also marks sounds like laughter or a song.
Goes in
The YouTube episode
Comes out
The video file, the audio, and the transcript (every word with start and end time)
Example (HM)
word:  "please."  from 1486.93 s to 1487.29 s, speaker_0
sound: [ఫోన్ రింగ్] (phone ringing) from 2:13.8 to 2:30.9

1 · Word fix

Gemini≈ ₹2–4
What it does
Finds words Scribe heard wrong. Gemini reads the whole transcript, then re-listens to the doubtful lines and fixes a word only when it clearly hears it.
Goes in
The transcript; for doubtful lines, a few seconds of their audio
Comes out
A list of corrected words. The original transcript is kept as it is.
Example (HM, L0209)
Scribe heard:  "pegs"
re-listened:   "ఓ రెండు పెగ్గులేసి పడుకుంటాం."
fixed to:      "పెగ్గులేసి"   (confidence 0.95)
↓

2 · Speaker fix

Gemini≈ ₹2
What it does
Scribe's speaker labels are often wrong. Gemini reads the dialogue and says who speaks each line, with a name when the dialogue gives one.
Goes in
The transcript (with the word fixes), in three overlapping parts
Comes out
For every line: a person key, a name, and how sure it is
Example (HM, 11:14)
Scribe:       speaker_4
speaker fix:  key G = Paddu (the mother), confidence 0.9

3 · Line table (Stage 1A)

code only₹0
What it does
Puts everything into one numbered list of lines (L0001, L0002 …): spoken lines and sound lines, in time order. It only measures; it decides nothing.
Goes in
Transcript + word fix + speaker fix
Comes out
The line table. CG has 404 lines (348 spoken), HM 464 (437 spoken).
Example (HM)
L0177  11:14.4 – 11:17.4  Paddu:  నేను ఇప్పుడు wine తాగుదాము అంటే ఏమంటావా అని

4 · Text step (Stage 1B)

Gemini1 call · ≈ ₹2.6
What it does
Reads the whole transcript and works out the people: names, roles, when two labels are the same person, and which parts are not story (ads, promos).
Goes in
The line table (text only, no video)
Comes out
The list of people, and the ad / promo parts
Example (HM)
key A = Hansini, "female protagonist / teacher"
  same person as key E
  evidence: L0001, L0015–L0027 (she presents a face-wash ad)

5 · Video step: who talks, who is on screen, the scenes

Gemini watches the whole episode1 call · ₹13–20
What it does
Gemini watches the episode (low quality, 1 picture per second, with sound). For every line it says who talks and who is on screen. It also describes each person's look and splits the episode into scenes. We don't give it scenes to check; it finds them itself, because it copied the scenes we used to send.
Goes in
The whole video, the line table, and the text step's people (as a guess to check)
Comes out
Per line: who talks, who is seen. People with their looks. Scenes (CG 17, HM 10, Surya 13).
Example (HM)
L0178 "ఏం మాట్లాడుతున్నావమ్మా":  talks = Hansini,  on screen = Hansini

Scene 8: L0158 – L0306 (10:07 – 17:44), Hansini's House
  why a scene:   Hansini and her mother Paddu talking in the living room and sharing wine
  why it ends:   night conversation ends as mother encourages her to give Aravind a chance

6 · Scene notes (Stage 2)

Gemini watches the whole episode1 call · ₹12–16
What it does
Describes every scene as plain facts: the story, what happens, the mood, reactions, what is seen without words, silences and music. It gives no opinions about hooks or payoffs (your rule: an opinion here would steer the next step).
Goes in
The whole video, the people with their looks, and the transcript split into the video step's scenes
Comes out
The episode's story in a paragraph, plus notes for every scene
Example (HM, scene 8, shortened)
story:     At home, Hansini warns her mother Paddu not to bring up Aravind, but Paddu
           surprises her by pouring wine and suggesting they drink like friends...
mood:      intimate, comedic, and deeply emotional
reaction:  after L0185, Hansini hesitates, awkward clinking glasses with her own mother
seen:      11:33.0 – 11:38.0  Paddu and Hansini clinking glasses of red wine
silence:   16:21.0 – 16:29.0  Hansini looks down, weighing her mother's words
           (music: tender emotional melody)

7 · Find clips (Stage 3 find)

Gemini, one call per scenenumbers: new₹32–61
What it does
Works like a Telugu regional editor, not from rules. For each scene it gives the clips whose payoff (the joke, reveal or emotional moment that lands) happens in that scene. A clip may start in an earlier scene.
How it's sent
The whole video plus all the text (people, story, scene notes, transcript) is stored at Google once. Each scene call reuses it at a tenth of the normal price and only says "Scene N".
Goes in
The video, people, story, scene notes, and the transcript with every word numbered and shot changes marked (‖)
Comes out
For each clip: where it lands, the start word, the end word plus "until" time, up to 3 whole lines to cut, a cold open (optional), hook, title, why it works, what the viewer feels
Example (CG): what Gemini sees
L0241 [11:19.2-11:21.8] Gowtham's friend | 751 మరి 752 ఇందుకేనా 753 నన్ను 754 పెళ్లి
      755 చేసుకున్నావ్ 756 అని 757 ఆ 758 అమ్మాయి 759 అడిగితే?
L0242 [11:22.0-11:28.6] (sound) [వేడుక గీతం]
   ‖ shot change 11:22.0
   ‖ shot change 11:28.4
L0243 [11:28.7-11:28.9] Amrutha's mother | 760 ఓసేయ్!
What it sends back
"start": {"word": 673, "why": "opens on 673 మనలాంటి 674 అబ్బాయిలకి … (boys like us …)"},
"end":   {"word": 759, "until": "11:28.4",
          "why": "holds on Gowtham's frozen face with the song until the cut to Amrutha's house"}

This answer is an example of the new format, written by hand; it hasn't been run yet. Code looks up word 759's time (ends 11:21.8), checks that 11:28.4 is after it and before the next spoken word (760 at 11:28.7), and plays the clip until 11:28.4. That's 6.6 s on the reaction.

8 · Edit clips (Stage 3 edit)

Gemini, one call per clipbeing tested≈ ₹2.5–3.6 per clip
What it does
An editor pass on each clip. Gemini watches only that clip's stretch (8 s before to 15 s after), at 2 pictures per second so it can see looks and nods. It cuts what slows the clip down, splits it if it's really two moments, and decides how long the end holds on the reaction.
Goes in
The clip's video, the scene's story, where the clip was found to land, and the transcript of the stretch (numbered words, shot changes, on the short video's own clock)
Comes out
The tightened clip (or two clips), with the same fields as step 7
Example (HM c07): before → after
found:   starts L0372, no cuts                                        79 s
edited:  starts L0383 "మా"  (Paddu's insight into Hansini's nature)
         cut L0387–L0393: awkward goodbye pleasantries
         ends L0406 "దండనండి." on Aravind's panic-bow                  41 s

Results so far: on HM it tightened 4 of 8 clips (c07 79→41 s, c08 97→45 s, c06 112→87 s, c01 37→32 s) but not c04 (138 s). On Surya it changed little. Your HM ratings decide whether this step stays.

The rules this flow follows

  • Gemini makes every editing decision (where a clip starts and ends, what to cut, how long to hold). Code only measures, looks up times, checks the answer's format, cuts video and keeps clips under 3 minutes.
  • Never hand Gemini an answer it can copy. It copied the scenes we sent and copied the previous step's lines, so each step gets facts, not decisions. The edit step is the deliberate exception: its job is to edit the clip it's given.
  • Think like a Telugu regional editor: the hook can be anywhere; a cliffhanger is one way to pull viewers, not the only one; clips under about 20 s aren't posted for these series.
  • Web series are cut mostly continuously: at most 3 cuts, each a whole chunk of lines, never words inside an actor's line.
  • Your ratings and approved clips are only for measuring; they never go into the pipeline.
